# KRYSYS Wholesale Research Audit (Internal)

This file documents the wholesale price benchmarks used to inform the KRYSYS catalog. **Never** link to, name, or reference the source sites on the customer-facing surface. These are private working data.

## Methodology

Prices gathered from public mineral/gem wholesale marketplaces and lapidary suppliers. Where a price range was returned, the **low-mid** wholesale figure was used as the catalog input, then a flat 10% markup was applied for the customer price.

## Coverage summary

- **273 crystals** across ~30 mineral families
- **17 geodes** including amethyst cathedrals, pyrite, celestite, septarian
- **83 of 118 periodic-table elements** (radioactive + synthetic excluded)

## Markup policy

All customer prices = wholesale × 1.10 (flat 10% markup). No subscription, no hidden fees.

## Notes on excluded elements

The following periodic elements are SKIPPED from the catalog because they cannot legally be sold or have no commercial source:

- **43 (Tc) Technetium** — radioactive, restricted
- **61 (Pm) Promethium** — radioactive, restricted
- **84-89 (Po, At, Rn, Fr, Ra, Ac)** — radioactive, illegal to sell
- **91 (Pa) Protactinium** — too rare
- **93-118** — radioactive or synthetic, no commercial source

Two elements ARE included with restrictions:
- **90 (Th) Thorium** — 25g sealed chunk, 99.9% pure, legal with note
- **92 (U) Uranium** — specimen-grade depleted uranium or autunite/carnotite, photo ID required in some regions
